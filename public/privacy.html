Privacy Policy

Bahr Network internal staff system · last updated 1 November 2026

This system is used only by Bahr Network and its staff to manage attendance, leave, tasks and team activity. It is not offered to the public.

What we store

Account details (name, username, department, job title, phone, birthday if given), attendance check-ins with the location used to confirm you are at the office, points, leave and correction requests, tasks, comments, kudos and announcements, and a hash of each device cookie used for sign-in security. Payroll figures are visible only to owners and to the employee concerned.

Google Drive

If the company owner connects a Google account, the system uses Google's drive.file permission. That lets it create folders and upload the files staff attach to tasks, and read only those files. It cannot see or change any other files in that Google Drive. Uploaded files are shared by link so teammates can open them. The connection can be removed at any time from the system's Settings or from the Google account's security page.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to store and show task files inside this system, and is never sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Who can see it

Owners can see all company data. Team leads see their own department's attendance and tasks. Employees see their own records and shared team spaces (wall, leaderboard, people). Data is never sold or shared outside the company.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in the company's database and, for task files, in the company's Google Drive. Owners can export or delete it at any time.

Contact

For any question or request about your data, contact the Bahr Network management.
